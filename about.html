About the café

Local meals with a simpler way to order.

Ous Ma'mikis WhatsApp Café is a small food business serving prepared meals to customers in the surrounding area.

Business background

The café currently uses WhatsApp to communicate with customers, share menu information and receive food orders. A significant customer group is staff working at nearby companies that do not have their own canteens.

The proposed website adds a convenient browsing and enquiry channel while keeping WhatsApp available for customers who prefer it.

Proposed mission

To prepare convenient, satisfying home-style meals and make ordering simple for local customers and nearby office workers.

Proposed vision

To become a trusted local lunch option known for approachable service, easy ordering and dependable prepared meals.

Target audience

  • Staff at nearby workplaces without canteens
  • Office workers in the surrounding area
  • Regular local customers
  • Customers seeking convenient prepared meals

Website goals

Make information easier to access

Show meals, sample prices and images without requiring a customer to first request a menu.

Support more enquiries

Provide clear calls to action for WhatsApp and online enquiry forms.

Improve the online presence

Give the small business a professional, mobile-friendly web presence that can be shared with customers.
